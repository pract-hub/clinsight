NCT05584111 Phase 1 Completed

Oral Administration of STC-15 in Subjects With Advanced Malignancies

Sponsor: STORM Therapeutics LTD · Enrollment: N = 42 (actual) · Footprint: 3 sites in 1 country (United States)

Trial Synopsis

NCT05584111 (STORM Therapeutics LTD) is a completed Phase 1 trial (N = 42 (actual)) evaluating STC-15 in Advanced Cancer; Advanced Solid Tumor; Cancer. The study comprises 5 designated cohorts/arms using a 3+3 + Dose Expansion (modified Fibonacci) design across 3 sites in 1 country (United States). A separate DLT evaluation window is not specified in the registry outcome measures. Primary outcomes evaluate: Number of participants with adverse events (Screening through end of treatment, approximately 6 months); Cmax (PK) (Screening through Cycle 2 (each cycle is 21 days)); Tmax (PK) (Screening through Cycle 2 (each cycle is 21 days)); plus 7 additional PK/safety measures.

Study Design, Cohorts & Endpoints
Escalation & Expansion
3+3 + Dose Expansion (modified Fibonacci)
Cohorts / Arms (5)
  • Dose Level 1: 30mg capsules, daily administration for 3 week (21 day) cycles
  • Dose Level 2: 30mg capsules, MWF administration for 3 week (21 day) cycles
  • Dose Level 3: 100mg capsules, MWF administration for 3 week (21 day) cycles
  • Dose Level 4: 30mg and 100mg capsules, M-MWF administration for 3 week (21 day) cycles
  • Dose Level 5: 30mg and 100mg capsules, M-MWF administration for 3 week (21 day) cycles

  • Tmax (PK): To determine the time to Cmax (Tmax) Timeframe: Screening through Cycle 2 (each cycle is 21 days)

  • Terminal elimination half life (PK): To determine the terminal elimination half-life (t½) Timeframe: Screening through Cycle 2 (each cycle is 21 days)

  • Volume of distribution at steady-state (PK): To determine the volume of distribution at steady-state (Vss) Timeframe: Screening through Cycle 2 (each cycle is 21 days)

Verbatim Registry Eligibility Criteria
Key Inclusion Criteria:

* \> 18 years of age
* Histologic or cytologic confirmation of advanced malignancy that has failed standard of care (SOC) therapy and no further SOC therapy is available or the subject has declined additional SOC therapy
* Adequate organ and marrow function
* ECOG PS of 0 or 1

Key Exclusion Criteria:

* Treatment with any local or systemic antineoplastic therapy within 3 weeks prior to first dose of STC-15
* Major surgery or radiation within the 3 weeks
* Immune-related AEs from immunotherapy that required permanent discontinuation
* Central nervous system (CNS) disease involvement, or prior history of Grade ≥3 drug-related CNS toxicity.
* Active autoimmune disease that has required systemic treatment in the 2 years prior to Screening
