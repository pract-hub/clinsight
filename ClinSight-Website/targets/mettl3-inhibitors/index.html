Class Overview (Generated from Verified Table Fields)

Three interventional clinical trials evaluating oral small-molecule METTL3 RNA methyltransferase inhibitors are registered on ClinicalTrials.gov across two investigational agents: STORM Therapeutics' STC-15 (NCT05584111, NCT06975293) and Epics Therapeutics' EP102 (NCT07163325). STC-15 completed its first-in-human Phase 1 monotherapy trial (NCT05584111; N = 42 (actual); 5 dose-escalation cohorts using a 3+3 + Dose Expansion (modified Fibonacci) design across 3 sites in 1 country (United States)) and is recruiting a Phase 1/2 study (NCT06975293; N = 107 (estimated); 3 cohorts using a 3+3 + Simon's 2-stage design across 14 sites in 1 country (United States)) evaluating STC-15 in combination with toripalimab, STC-15 monotherapy in Solid Tumor; Sarcoma; Leiomyosarcoma; Dedifferentiated Liposarcoma. Epics Therapeutics' EP102 is recruiting a first-in-human Phase 1 monotherapy study (NCT07163325; N = 48 (estimated); 5 dose levels using a BOIN design across 11 sites in 4 countries (Belgium, Czechia, Netherlands, Spain)) with a specified DLT assessment window of 'Up to 21 Days after first administration'.

Phase 1/2 Recruiting

STC-15 as a Part of Combination Therapy With Toripalimab in Selected Advanced Cancers and as Monotherapy in Participants With Selected Sarcomas

STORM Therapeutics LTD · N = 107 (estimated) · 14 sites in 1 country (United States)

NCT06975293 (STORM Therapeutics LTD) is a recruiting Phase 1/2 trial (N = 107 (estimated)) evaluating STC-15 in combination with toripalimab, STC-15 monotherapy in Solid Tumor; Sarcoma; Leiomyosarcoma; Dedifferentiated Liposarcoma. The study comprises 3 designated cohorts/arms using a 3+3 + Simon's 2-stage design across 14 sites in 1 country (United States). A separate DLT evaluation window is not specified in the registry outcome measures. Primary outcomes evaluate: Combination Cohorts: Safety and tolerability of STC-15 in combination with toripalimab (6 months); Phase 2 Monotherapy Cohorts: Antitumor activity of STC-15 monotherapy (6 months).
